# Hollowmere: character bible

Everyone in town, in depth. Sprites: [concept-art/town/town_lineup.png](concept-art/town/town_lineup.png) · quick look table: [concept-art/town/CAST.md](concept-art/town/CAST.md) · family tree: [townsfolk.png](townsfolk.png).

Each entry covers **who they are**, **what they want and fear**, **the contradiction or secret** that makes them more than their job, **their ties**, **their arc**, **what they do in the game**, and **how they sound**.

---

## The big tensions in town

These run through many people at once. They're where most of the drama comes from.

1. **The Abbey and the Hedge.** Father Aldous says only the Church can make the graveyard soil safe. Sabine Marsh says the soil doesn't need a blessing, it needs *listening to*. Years ago Sabine's husband died of fever after Aldous forbade her remedies, and neither of them has forgiven the other. Now they're trapped together in the Abbey crypt, and the town's food depends on both of them. (Mechanics: [GAME_DESIGN.md §23](GAME_DESIGN.md).)
2. **Those who went and those who stayed.** Most able adults followed Captain Percival into the ambush. The few who stayed behind (Holt, ordered to; Gus, held back by Nell) carry a different guilt from the ones who went.
3. **What people did to survive in the dungeon.** Edric carved for the Dungeon Master. Bruna runs his machines. Tam was offered a real sword. Rescues come home with shame attached.
4. **Debts.** Cordelia carried the town ledger into the wilds and still believes every debt must be paid. The game's answer, that shared hope is a gift and not a debt, has to get past her.
5. **Hope as a danger.** Hester taught her daughters that hope is a debt. Mira believes it. Pip doesn't. The Dungeon Master is counting on all three.
6. **The door.** Ignatius Thorne may be the reason the Dungeon Master found Hollowmere at all. He hasn't told anyone.

## The Pells

### Father Aldous: Priest (72) · hiding in the Abbey crypt
*A man who built a wall out of faith and stands on it every day.*
- **Look:** Gaunt and straight-backed, a white fringe of hair, black cassock, white collar. Holds his scripture like a shield and raises a finger instead of a hand.
- **Personality:** Steadfast, severe, tireless. He doesn't comfort; he commands. He believes order is mercy: rules keep people alive when feelings won't. He's harsh with everyone, and hardest on himself.
- **Wants:** To hold Hollowmere under God's order in a world that has no God in it.
- **Fears:** That his prayers don't reach anyone here. The blessings still *work*, but they feel different, colder, and he doesn't know who is answering.
- **Contradiction / secret:** Eighteen years ago he forbade Sabine's fever remedies and told Corwin Marsh to trust in prayer. Corwin died. Aldous says it was God's will. Privately he keeps a small book with the name of every soul he couldn't save, and Corwin's name is underlined.
- **Ties:** Younger brother of **Hester**, who raised him and says he grew up "exactly like our father". **Brother Fennick**, his novice, gets the worst of his temper. **Sabine** is his enemy. **Winnie** grew up in his Abbey orphanage and still flinches at his voice.
- **Arc:** From rigid to humble, but never soft. The turning point is the night his blessing alone isn't enough and he has to ask Sabine, in front of the town, to stand with him. He does it badly and means it.
- **In the game:** Rescuing him unlocks **Consecration** (blessed soil, blessed borders, the Abbey bell as a hope anchor). He insists the Strange Garden be moved next to the Abbey "so it stays where God and I can watch it".
- **Voice:** *"Faith is not a feeling. It is a wall. We build it every morning or we die outside it."*

### Hester Pell: Retired midwife (68) · in the wilds
*She delivered half the town and she can tell when any of them are lying.*
- **Look:** Small, grey topknot, plum dress, blue-grey shawl, a cane she pretends is for show.
- **Personality:** Sharp, funny, merciless with fools, endlessly patient with the frightened. The real morale leader of the group in the wilds, though Holt has the title.
- **Wants:** Her daughters and grandchildren safe, and the last word in every argument.
- **Fears:** Being useless.
- **Contradiction / secret:** She "retired" because her hands shake and her eyes are going. She hasn't told anyone. It was Hester who taught Mira *"hope is a debt"*, the night her husband Tobias drowned and the whole town spent three days hoping. She has regretted those words for twenty-two years.
- **Ties:** Mother of **Mira** and **Martha**, grandmother of **Pip**, **Kit** and **Kat**. Older sister of **Aldous**: they haven't had an honest conversation since their father's funeral. An old ally of **Sabine** (midwives and herb-women always stood together).
- **Arc:** Undoing her own lesson. Her quiet goal is to hear Mira say she hopes again, and to be the one who taught her that too.
- **In the game:** Reads people: she tells you who is lying, who is struggling and who needs a visit (a relationship-hint system). Sides with Sabine in the soil conflict.
- **Voice:** *"He's a farmer, your Dungeon Master. I was married to a fisherman. I know men who wait for things to grow."*

### Tobias Pell † (died 22 years ago)
Fisherman, Hester's husband. Drowned when Mira was twelve. The town searched for three days, hoping, and found him on the fourth. The reason the Pell women are the way they are.

## The Wainwrights

### Mira Wainwright: Baker (34) · with you
*She keeps going by refusing to want anything.*
- **Look:** Light brown hair in a low bun with strands always escaping, brown eyes, plain teal dress, felt hat with a wheat sprig outdoors. No apron until she bakes again.
- **Personality:** Practical, sharp-tongued, controlling, very funny when she forgets herself. Does everything herself because accepting help means hoping someone will stay.
- **Wants:** Pip safe. Everything else is negotiable.
- **Fears:** Hoping, and being made a fool of by it again.
- **Contradiction / secret:** *She* convinced Edric to go on the expedition: "Someone sensible needs to keep an eye on the Captain." If he's dead, it's her fault. That's why she can't hope: hope means admitting she wants back what she sent away.
- **Ties:** **Edric** (husband, missing), **Pip** (son), **Hester** (mother), **Martha** (sister she envies and adores), **Oswin** (she nurses him with the grim tenderness she won't show herself).
- **Arc:** From "Don't you dare tell my son his father is coming back" to tying on her apron, to *"Fine. I hope. Don't make me regret it."* (Full story: GAME_DESIGN.md §20.)
- **In the game:** Reopening her bakery: daily bread (energy) and shared meals (a hope anchor).
- **Voice:** *"Hope is a debt. Someone always comes to collect."*

### Edric Wainwright: Carpenter, toymaker (36) · in the dungeon (the Cellar)
*The kindest man in town, and he did something in the dark he can't take back.*
- **Look:** Tall, dark brown hair parted in the middle, short beard, Pip's green eyes, leather vest, rolled sleeves, always a pencil behind his ear.
- **Personality:** Warm, whistling, a dreamer who can't say no to anyone. Conflict-avoidant to a fault: he went on the expedition because both Percival and Mira asked, and he couldn't choose.
- **Wants:** To come home and pretend none of this happened.
- **Fears:** That Pip will see him differently.
- **Contradiction / secret:** The Dungeon Master is keeping him hopeful, "aging him like wine". In return for being allowed to send letters home, Edric **carved the figures for the Dungeon Master's table**: little wooden townsfolk, every one of them someone he knows, for the Dungeon Master to play with. He made them beautifully. He couldn't help it.
- **Ties:** **Mira**, **Pip**, his tools. **Percival** (they were in the dungeon together; Edric is the only one who never laughed at him).
- **Arc:** The rescue isn't the end of his story; the confession is. The reunion is the first failed Harvest. The harder scene comes weeks later, when Pip finds a carved figure of himself in the dungeon.
- **In the game:** His workshop: carpentry, building upgrades, and toys (gifts that raise hope). Knows the Cellar from the inside.
- **Voice:** *"I told myself if I made them well enough, he'd treat them gently. That's the thing about hoping. It makes you stupid in such a reasonable way."*

### Pip Wainwright (7) · with you
*The most hopeful person in Hollowmere, which makes him the most valuable thing in it.*
- **Look:** Messy light brown hair with a cowlick (Mira's), big green eyes (Edric's), freckles, sticking-out ears, rust tunic with a mended tear, suspenders. Never without his lantern.
- **Personality:** Brave in the wrong moments, curious about everything strange, a talented liar when he thinks it will make his mother feel better.
- **Wants:** Dad home. Also to be taken seriously.
- **Fears:** The dark under his bed, where something sometimes moves. (It's a root.)
- **Contradiction / secret:** He's angry at his mother for not believing, and he hides it under being good. He can **see hope**: a faint gold shimmer on people and things. He hasn't told anyone because grown-ups don't believe him.
- **Ties:** **Sprout** (the root creature he adopted), **Juniper** (best friend), the **Cobb twins** (heroes, in his eyes), **Mira**, **Edric**.
- **Arc:** Learning that hoping doesn't mean pretending. He has to be allowed to be scared.
- **In the game:** His lantern in the window is the first hope anchor. His sketchbook is the quest log. With Sprout he can point out where loose hope is leaking.
- **Voice:** *"Sprout's not a monster. Monsters don't like carrots."*

## The Cobbs

### Martha Cobb: Seamstress, town gossip (38) · in the wilds
*Knows everyone's secrets and uses them to keep the peace.*
- **Look:** Light brown curls (the Pell hair), rose bonnet, mustard dress, pink shawl, tape measure around her neck, hands on hips.
- **Personality:** Warm, loud, nosy, kind. Gossip is how she manages her anxiety: if she knows everything, nothing can surprise her.
- **Wants:** Everybody to get along, by force if necessary.
- **Fears:** Silence. Being the only one who doesn't know something.
- **Contradiction / secret:** She's the real brain of the Cobb shop; Hamish just stands behind the counter. She envies Mira's quiet dignity; Mira envies her warmth. She has quietly settled half the town's feuds by telling each side a slightly different version of the truth.
- **Ties:** **Hamish** (husband), **Kit** and **Kat** (twins she can't control), **Mira** (sister), **Hester** (mother).
- **Arc:** Learning to sit with not knowing, out in the wilds where gossip can't fix anything.
- **In the game:** Tailoring (clothes, warmth, festival outfits). As a gossip she tells you rumours: some useful, some wrong.
- **Voice:** *"I'm not saying anything. I'm just saying."*

### Hamish Cobb: Provisioner (42) · hiding in the Granary
*Barricaded himself in with the food. He'll tell you it was for the town.*
- **Look:** Big belly, ginger handlebar moustache, balding, green waistcoat, shop apron, clutching his coin purse.
- **Personality:** Penny-pinching, fussy, comic, a coward with a strong sense of duty.
- **Wants:** Stock. Ledgers that balance. To be respected.
- **Fears:** Going hungry. He grew up in a famine year and has never forgotten the taste of bark bread.
- **Contradiction / secret:** He has been slightly under-weighting flour for years. **Cordelia** knows. He did barricade himself in the Granary with the nest to save the grain: he really was protecting it for everyone. Both things are true.
- **Ties:** **Martha** (wife), the twins, **Cordelia** (nemesis).
- **Arc:** The miser who gives everything away at a Harvest feast. Comic until that moment, then the most moving scene of his life.
- **In the game:** Shop and storage; a buy/sell economy; haggling.
- **Voice:** *"Free? Nothing's free. Things cost. Even this. Especially this. …Oh, take it, then."*

### Kit Cobb (12) · in the wilds
*Fires first, thinks never.*
- **Look:** Ginger, freckles, flat cap, blue tunic, slingshot always loaded.
- **Personality:** Reckless, loud, wants to be Tam Fletcher. Protective of his sister to the point of stupidity.
- **Fears:** The dark. He sleeps with his slingshot.
- **Secret:** He knows Kat is cleverer than him and is proud of it.
- **Ties:** **Kat**, **Pip** (who follows him around), **Tam** (hero).
- **In the game:** Small errands and scouting; a minigame with the slingshot (pests, crows; never Parson).
- **Voice:** *"I wasn't scared. I was being careful loudly."*

### Kat Cobb (12) · in the wilds
*The quiet twin, and the one who plans everything.*
- **Look:** Ginger pigtails with red ribbons, freckles, green dress, an apple in her hand that she didn't pay for.
- **Personality:** Clever, sly, generous with stolen things. She steals for people who need it (food for Bram, a blanket for Hester).
- **Wants:** To go on expeditions. To see the strange world.
- **Secret:** She has been keeping a map of the wilds, more accurate than Holt's.
- **Ties:** **Kit**, **Juniper** (rivals), **Hester** (the only adult she can't fool).
- **In the game:** Her map improves the exploration map; she can "find" things (light thief work).
- **Voice:** *"It's not stealing if it was going to be eaten by a monster anyway."*

## The Dunmores

### Ambrose Dunmore: Miller (74) · lost in the Old Mill fog
*For him it is still Harvest Fair night, and he's having a wonderful time.*
- **Look:** White beard, floppy miller's cap, everything dusted with flour, a flour sack on his shoulder.
- **Personality:** Jolly, stubborn, deaf in one ear (he picks which one depending on who's talking).
- **Contradiction:** In the fog he relives the best night of the year, over and over, perfectly happy. Freeing him means bringing him into a ruined world.
- **Ties:** **Hilde** (wife of fifty years), **Nell** (daughter), **Tilly** (granddaughter).
- **In the game:** The mill: flour (bread, baking, feasts).
- **Voice:** *"Another round for the fair! What do you mean, which fair?"*

### Hilde Dunmore: Miller (72) · lost in the Old Mill fog
*She knows it isn't real. She's choosing to stay.*
- **Look:** White linen coif, lavender dress, apron, always knitting.
- **Personality:** Sharp memory, dry humour, the brains of the mill.
- **Secret:** Hilde half-remembers the truth inside the fog and has decided not to look at it, because outside Ambrose would have to grieve. The fog is a **jar of preserved hope**: the Dungeon Master's pantry.
- **Ties:** **Ambrose**, **Nell**.
- **Arc / choice:** Freeing them is a real moral choice. You need her consent, and she'll only give it if Nell asks her herself.
- **Voice:** *"Let him have one more dance, love. Then we'll talk about the end of the world."*

## The Brannocks

### Gus Brannock: Innkeeper (48) · hiding in the Tankard cellar
*The loudest laugh in town, and an old axe under the bar.*
- **Look:** Huge, bald, grey mutton chops, rolled sleeves, apron, always raising a tankard.
- **Personality:** Big-hearted host, terrible singer, the man who first tells you how bad things are.
- **Contradiction / secret:** Before Hollowmere, Gus was a sellsword with **Holt**. He swore off fighting after something he did in a war nobody here remembers. When Tilly was taken he wanted to charge into the dungeon alone; Nell stopped him at the cellar door. He's more afraid of himself than of monsters.
- **Ties:** **Nell** (wife), **Tilly** (daughter), **Holt** (old comrade), the whole town (his customers, his family).
- **Arc:** Picking the axe up again, on his own terms, to bring Tilly home.
- **In the game:** The Tankard is the story hub. Later he can join dungeon trips as a heavy fighter.
- **Voice:** *"First one's on the house. The second one, you tell me what's wrong."*

### Nell Brannock: Brewer, cook (45) · hiding in the Tankard cellar
*Runs the Tankard. Lets Gus think he does.*
- **Look:** Green kerchief over black curls, red dress, rolled sleeves, apron, ladle in hand.
- **Personality:** Practical, devout, warm-tough, never sits down.
- **Contradiction:** She sides with **Aldous** in everything, but **Sabine** saved Tilly from fever as a baby when the Abbey's prayers didn't. Nell pays Sabine back in quiet bread deliveries and never mentions it.
- **Fears:** Her parents lost in the fog, her daughter in the dungeon: she cooks so she doesn't have to think.
- **Ties:** **Gus**, **Tilly**, her parents the **Dunmores**.
- **In the game:** Brewery & kitchen: ale, meals, recipes (energy and hope).
- **Voice:** *"Eat. Then cry. In that order, it works better."*

### Tilly Brannock: Barmaid (16) · in the dungeon (the Granary)
*The one prisoner the Dungeon Master can't farm.*
- **Look:** Black ponytail with a red ribbon, red bodice, navy skirt, a tray of mugs.
- **Personality:** Quick, cynical, funny, allergic to being rescued.
- **Contradiction:** She hopes in *plans*, not feelings, and the Dungeon Master finds her bland. She's been planning her own escape for weeks, and when you arrive, she's annoyed you got there first.
- **Ties:** **Tam** (captured too; she's been keeping him alive), **Linden** (she knows he likes her; she's leaving town the moment she can, and the world outside is now full of monsters, which is very inconvenient).
- **In the game:** A rescue that becomes a partnership: her escape plan is half the route out of the Granary level.
- **Voice:** *"You're the rescue? Great. Hold this. And don't touch anything shiny."*

## The Varrs

### Captain Percival Varr: Captain of the guard (40) · in the dungeon (everywhere)
*A brave man who's terrible at being brave.*
- **Look:** Black hair slicked back, pale skin, thin moustache, navy coat, red sash, medals, plumed bicorne, sword at his hip. (Later: the same coat, in rags.)
- **Personality:** Pompous, theatrical, desperate to be admired.
- **Contradiction:** His father **Aurelian** called him "ornamental" his whole life. He led the expedition partly out of vanity and partly out of genuine terror for the town; he really did think he was saving everyone. The Dungeon Master gives him an "escape chance" every day as an appetizer. He keeps trying. Every time. That's the brave part.
- **Ties:** **Cordelia** (the competent sister he resents and adores), **Winnie** (the only person who's seen him without his medals), **Holt** (whom he left behind as a punishment for questioning the plan).
- **Arc:** From pompous to humble. Back in town he's broken; Winnie looks after him; their roles flip. The best version of him ends up as a gate guard who apologises to everyone, one by one.
- **In the game:** A recurring encounter on every dungeon level. Carries the town's keyring.
- **Voice (early):** *"Citizens! We shall be home by supper!"* · **(late):** *"I'm sorry. I'm sorry. I'll keep saying it until it means something."*

### Cordelia Varr: Town treasurer (37) · in the wilds
*Took the town ledger into the wilderness because someone has to remember what's owed.*
- **Look:** Neat black updo, spectacles, high-collared dark green dress, the ledger under her arm.
- **Personality:** Cold, exact, fair to the penny, privately funny.
- **Contradiction / secret:** She ran the Counting House while her brother got the titles, and **she approved the treasury money for the expedition**. She carries the ledger because order is the only thing she can control, and because the last line in it is her signature.
- **Ties:** **Percival**, **Winnie** (employer, almost friend), **Hamish** (she knows about the flour), **Holt's group**.
- **Arc:** The **Ledger of Debts** made into a person. Her big moment is tearing pages out of the ledger, forgiving the town's debts as an act of shared hope, which the Dungeon Master can't harvest.
- **In the game:** The Counting House: the ledger, the economy, and the record of every Dungeon Master offer you accept.
- **Voice:** *"Everything is owed to someone. The question is only whether we decide to collect."*

### Winnie Crane: Maid, hireable (29) · hiding in the Counting House
*Loyal to the household. Not to anyone's ego.*
- **Look:** Dark bob, frilled maid's cap, black dress, white apron, a feather duster held like a sword.
- **Personality:** Dry, quick, unflappable, quietly ambitious.
- **Contradiction / secret:** A foundling raised in **Aldous's** orphanage; she still flinches at his voice and still says grace. She dreams of a tea shop of her own and has been saving for it for twelve years (the money is in the Counting House vault, which is locked).
- **Ties:** **Percival**, **Cordelia**, **Dorrit** (rival), **Aldous** (complicated).
- **In the game:** First hireable helper (21.1 in the design doc). Knows where the spare keys are.
- **Voice:** *"The Captain practised that speech on the stairs. Twice."*

## The Thornes

### Ignatius Thorne: Arcanist (80) · sealed in his tower
*Delighted by the strange world. He may have opened the door to it.*
- **Look:** Wild white hair, long beard, spectacles, starry blue robe, crooked pointed hat, a staff with a glowing crystal.
- **Personality:** Absent-minded, kind, endlessly curious, talks to himself and occasionally to the ceiling.
- **Contradiction / secret:** For a year before the Shift, he was studying strange glyphs that appeared in his scrying glass, and **he answered them**. He suspects that's how the Dungeon Master found Hollowmere. He hides the guilt behind cheerful dottiness, and the Dungeon Master's glyphs sealing his tower are, he thinks, a thank-you note.
- **Ties:** **Rowan** (son), **Elsa** (daughter-in-law, who blames his "dabbling" for everything), **Juniper** (granddaughter, who has his gift; he's terrified it will make her a target), **Lewin** (scholarly frenemy).
- **Arc:** Confession, and paying it back by teaching Juniper to use her gift to fight the Dungeon Master, not to call him.
- **In the game:** Arcane research; reading the Dungeon Master's glyphs.
- **Voice:** *"Fascinating! Terrible, yes, but fascinating."*

### Rowan Thorne: Farmer (39) · in the dungeon (the Roots)
*The roots feed on his love for the land. He's started to understand them.*
- **Look:** Straw hat, stubble, green vest, rolled sleeves, a pitchfork.
- **Personality:** Quiet, patient, more comfortable with soil than people.
- **Contradiction:** Half-entangled in the Roots level, he's been "talking" with the roots for weeks. He thinks they aren't evil, just hungry, like any crop. When you finally find a way to cut the roots, he may ask you not to.
- **Ties:** **Elsa**, **Juniper**, **Bram**, **Ignatius** (a father he loves and doesn't understand).
- **In the game:** Rescue him to unlock better farming; his knowledge of the roots is key to the long-term "cut them off at the root" goal, and he's the strongest voice for a gentler solution.
- **Voice:** *"Everything that grows is hungry. That's not the same as wicked."*

### Elsa Thorne: Farmer, orchard keeper (36) · in the wilds
*Holding a family together with a baby on her hip and her teeth gritted.*
- **Look:** Blonde braid over one shoulder, light blue dress, brown apron, a basket of apples.
- **Personality:** Warm, practical, fierce, short-tempered when tired (always).
- **Contradiction:** She sees Juniper's gift and tries to stamp it out, because of what Ignatius's "dabbling" cost them. It's the one place she's unkind.
- **Ties:** **Rowan**, **Juniper**, **Bram**, **Ignatius**.
- **In the game:** Orchard and fruit; grafting (a gentle cousin of the Mixing Bed).
- **Voice:** *"Magic. Wonderful. Can it change a nappy?"*

### Juniper Thorne (10) · in the wilds
*Bossy, brilliant, and something in the dark knows her name.*
- **Look:** Dark bob with a star clip, purple dress, a spark of magic in her palm.
- **Personality:** Confident, bossy, competitive with Kat, fiercely loyal to Pip.
- **Secret:** She sometimes hears "the tall man in the dark". He asks about Pip.
- **Ties:** **Pip** (best friend), **Ignatius**, **Elsa**, **Kat**.
- **In the game:** Later helper for arcane puzzles; with Pip and Sprout she can find root nodes.
- **Voice:** *"I'm not showing off. I'm showing."*

### Bram Thorne (baby) · in the wilds
The first child to say a word in the new world. It's "light". The whole town hears about it within the hour (Martha). A small, huge morale moment.

## The Fletchers

### Garrick Fletcher: Stablemaster (62) · hiding in the stables loft
*Better with frightened animals than with his own children.*
- **Look:** Grey beard, flat cap, red shirt, leather vest, a torch in the dark stable.
- **Personality:** Silent, gentle with animals, stiff with people. Widower.
- **Contradiction:** He's hiding above a frightened, half-changed ox he can't calm, the first animal that has ever refused him. He thinks he's lost his touch. He has; it's grief.
- **Ties:** **Wren** and **Tam** (children he raised without much warmth), the ox.
- **Arc:** Calming the ox teaches him to approach Tam, after the rescue, the way he'd approach a frightened animal: slowly, low, without asking anything.
- **In the game:** Stables, the ox and the mules (carts, heavy rubble, supply runs).
- **Voice:** *"Don't look it in the eye. Don't rush. Let it come to you. …Same goes for boys."*

### Wren Fletcher: Hunter (26) · with you
*Your first helper, and she hasn't decided whether to trust you.*
- **Look:** Green hood, black braid, olive tunic, quiver on her back, a bow always in hand.
- **Personality:** Stoic, competent, blunt, notices everything.
- **Contradiction:** She taught Tam to fight, so she blames herself for him sneaking onto the expedition. She's a pragmatist who'd leave Hollowmere tomorrow if staying stopped making sense, and she's waiting to see whether you make it make sense.
- **Ties:** **Tam**, **Garrick**, **Oswin** (she carried him home), **you**.
- **Arc:** From "I'll stay while it's useful" to loyalty.
- **In the game:** First companion: hunting, scouting, ranged fighting in the dungeon.
- **Voice:** *"I don't follow people. I walk next to them until they prove they know the way."*

### Tam Fletcher (17) · in the dungeon (the Press)
*Wanted to be a hero. The Dungeon Master offered him the chance.*
- **Look:** A cooking pot for a helmet, a padded vest, a wooden sword and a pot-lid shield.
- **Personality:** Eager, brave, naive, desperate to matter.
- **Contradiction:** In the Press, the Dungeon Master offered him a real sword and a hero's role in his "games". Tam is tempted, and his hope of being a hero is exactly what is being farmed. **Tilly** has been talking him out of it.
- **Ties:** **Wren**, **Garrick**, **Tilly**, **Kit** (who worships him).
- **Arc:** Learning that being useful beats being a hero. A possible dark branch if the player is careless.
- **Voice:** *"I'm not scared! …Is that a normal noise? For a wall?"*

## The Hammersleys

### Bruna Hammersley: Master smith (50) · in the dungeon (the Press)
*Forced to run the Dungeon Master's machines. Secretly, she's good at it.*
- **Look:** Red bandana, short auburn hair, ruddy face, bare forearms, leather apron, hammer raised.
- **Personality:** Gruff, proud, fierce, rarely praises anyone.
- **Contradiction:** She keeps the Press running slow on purpose, a quiet sabotage. But the machinery is the most beautiful work she's ever seen, and some days she forgets to sabotage it.
- **Ties:** **Dag** (husband), **Oswin** (foster son, whom she pushed away during the ambush to save him; he believes it was rejection).
- **Arc:** Coming home and finally telling Oswin he's the best thing she ever made.
- **In the game:** Rescue her to unlock master smithing.
- **Voice:** *"Fine work. Shame about who it's for."*

### Dag Hammersley: Armorer (52) · in the wilds
*Makes things that keep people alive. Refuses to make anything else.*
- **Look:** Bald, full dark beard, mail shirt, a shield he's always mending.
- **Personality:** Soft-spoken gentle giant, slow to anger, slow to everything.
- **Contradiction:** A pacifist armorer: armour and shields, never weapons. In a world full of monsters, that principle gets tested every day, and the town (and Bruna) need weapons.
- **Ties:** **Bruna**, **Oswin**.
- **In the game:** Armour and shields; refuses to forge weapons until a late personal quest.
- **Voice:** *"A sword is a question. A shield is an answer."*

### Oswin: Smith's apprentice (19) · with you, badly injured
*He ran when they took her. He can't stop replaying it.*
- **Look:** Blond, bandaged head, arm in a sling, a wince he tries to hide.
- **Personality:** Earnest, proud, angry during recovery, a quiet joker when well.
- **Contradiction / secret:** Survivor's guilt. Bruna shoved him away and ordered him to run. He tells everyone she saved him; inside he believes she was getting rid of him.
- **Ties:** **Bruna**, **Dag** (foster parents), **Mira** (nurses him), **Wren** (carried him home).
- **Arc:** Recovery (time and care), then relighting the forge, which leads to vine cutters, which open the fields. His healing literally opens the town.
- **Voice:** *"I'm fine. That's not blood, that's… mostly not blood."*

## The Marshes

### Sabine Marsh: Herbalist, healer, soothsayer (47) · hiding in the Abbey crypt
*Grim, unafraid, and right about the soil.*
- **Look:** Tall and thin, long black hair with one white streak, pale grey eyes, a near-black dress and dark shawl, bone charms on a cord, belt of herb pouches, a bundle of dried grave-herbs, and **Parson**, her crow, on her shoulder. (She named him to annoy Aldous.)
- **Personality:** Dry, blunt, unsentimental with adults, endlessly gentle with sick children. Reads omens in ash, bones and the way crows fly. Doesn't hate God; hates certainty.
- **Wants:** To be proven right without having to say "I told you so". (She will say it.)
- **Fears:** Being the last of her line. Her mother was a hedge-witch from the marshes; Linden doesn't want the gift.
- **Contradiction / secret:** **She saw the Shift coming in the bones a week before it happened**, and told no one, because who listens to the witch? She has carried that silence like a stone. And during the ambush she and Aldous kept each other alive in the crypt for weeks: she set his broken wrist; he gave her his blanket. Neither will speak of it.
- **Ties:** **Aldous** (enemy; Corwin's death), **Linden** (son), **Hester** (old ally), **Nell** (whose daughter she once saved), **Fennick** (who secretly trades notes with her), **Corwin †**.
- **Arc:** From bitter outsider to the other pillar of the town. The soil needs both of them.
- **In the game:** Medicine (Oswin's recovery), the **Settling** rite for the graveyard soil (GAME_DESIGN.md §23), and soothsaying: hints about coming Harvest nights and where the roots will strike next.
- **Voice:** *"Your priest blesses the ground. I ask it what it wants. One of us is listening."*

### Corwin Marsh † (died 18 years ago, when Linden was a baby)
Sabine's husband, a gentle, devout weaver. During the fever year he chose Aldous's counsel over his wife's remedies, and died. Both of them have been fighting over his memory ever since.

### Linden Marsh: Herb gardener (19) · in the wilds
*Half his mother's son, half the Abbey's, and hiding both.*
- **Look:** Long fringe over one eye, pale green shirt, garden apron, a watering can.
- **Personality:** Shy, kind, plant-whisperer, says almost nothing.
- **Contradiction / secret:** Sabine sent him to the Abbey school so he'd be accepted; he took to it, and she feels betrayed. He also has her gift (his dreams come true) and hides it from both her and Aldous.
- **Ties:** **Sabine**, **Tilly** (in love; never said it), **Fennick** (friend from Abbey school), **Elsa** (teaches him grafting in the wilds).
- **Arc:** Accepting both halves of himself. His gift shows the player future Harvest nights in dreams.
- **In the game:** Herb Garden; normal-soil herbs for medicine; helps keep the soils separate.
- **Voice:** *"Plants don't mind if you don't talk. That's why I like them."*

## The Barrows

### Sergeant Holt Barrow: Guard sergeant (55) · in the wilds
*The only guard left behind. He saved more people than anyone.*
- **Look:** Kettle helmet, grey beard, mail with the blue town tabard, sword and shield.
- **Personality:** Grim humour, bad knees, the steadiest person in a crisis.
- **Contradiction:** Percival left him behind as punishment for questioning the plan. When the raid came, Holt led everyone he could out of the Wilds Gate. He's ashamed he wasn't on the expedition, and secretly, shamefully, relieved.
- **Ties:** **Lewin** (husband), **Gus** (old comrade from their sellsword days), **Percival** (whom he'd like to punch and then hug).
- **In the game:** The wilds group's leader; later, town defence and training at the Guild Hall.
- **Voice:** *"Orders were to stay. So I stayed. Then I left. Both were right."*

### Lewin Barrow: Teacher, scribe, town chronicler (50) · in the wilds
*Writing everything down, in case nobody remembers Hollowmere existed.*
- **Look:** Wine beret, spectacles, long brown coat, an oil lamp to write by.
- **Personality:** Gentle, precise, sentimental about books.
- **Contradiction / secret:** In the ruins of the other towns he's found their chronicles too, and every one of them **stops on the same kind of night**: the night of their great harvest feast. He hasn't told anyone what he thinks that means.
- **Ties:** **Holt**, **Ignatius** (they argue about everything), the children (his former pupils).
- **In the game:** Research help; the town chronicle as a lore/collection log; the first person to understand the Harvest pattern.
- **Voice:** *"If we vanish, someone should know we were here, and that we were kind."*

## The Abbey

### Brother Fennick: Novice (22) · hiding in the Abbey crypt
*Losing his faith, finding something else, terrified of being caught.*
- **Look:** Tonsure, brown habit, rope belt, praying nervously over a candle; always slightly sweaty.
- **Personality:** Anxious, sweet, comic, a better person than he thinks.
- **Contradiction / secret:** He's been secretly trading notes with **Sabine** about herbs, omens and old rites for two years. In the crypt he watched the two people he respects most refuse to speak to each other for weeks. He's the bridge, if he can stop shaking.
- **Ties:** **Aldous** (terrifying master), **Sabine** (secret teacher), **Linden** (school friend).
- **Arc:** From go-between to the person who proposes the **double rite**, and stands between Aldous and Sabine while they argue about it.
- **In the game:** The Abbey's daily work, candles, and the go-between for the soil conflict.
- **Voice:** *"Father, with respect, and please don't shout, but what if she's also right?"*

## Your household

### You: the player (35) · with you
*The wealthy resident of Hollowmere. The Dungeon Master spared you on purpose.*
- **Look:** Customisable. Concept default: a good green coat over a waistcoat, a shovel in hand.
- **Deliberately open:** Your personality comes from your choices. The fixed facts: you're comfortably off, you went on the expedition, and you were let go. *"Go home, little shepherd. Grow something for me."*
- **In the game:** Everything.

### Dorrit: Your housekeeper (66) · hiding in your manor's cellar
*Treats the end of the world as a disruption to the household schedule.*
- **Look:** White topknot, navy dress, lace collar, apron, a pot of tea.
- **Personality:** Proper, unflappable, quietly terrifying.
- **Contradiction / secret:** She has served your family for forty years and knows things about how its fortune was made that you don't. She'll tell you when she decides you've earned it.
- **Ties:** **You**, **Winnie** (rival: "that girl irons with her opinions").
- **In the game:** Found when you reclaim your manor. Runs the household: breakfast, storage, and the occasional devastating piece of advice.
- **Voice:** *"Monsters at the gate. Very well. Tea first."*
