# gam — working title: *Hollowmere*

A cozy-but-dark top-down pixel-art game for Steam, in the spirit of *Stardew Valley* and *Graveyard Keeper*.
Your town has been pulled into a hostile world. A Dungeon Master who farms hope wants you to rebuild it,
so he can harvest it. Reclaim Hollowmere by day, face the dungeon by night, and break his chain.
